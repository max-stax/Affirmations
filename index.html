Affirmations

    Lines that are simply true for you now. Restore one if you want it back in rotation.

      Daily reminder (iPhone Shortcut)
      1. Open Shortcuts → Automation → + → Time of Day. Pick your time, choose Run Immediately.
      2. Add the action Open App and choose Affirm (this app, once it's on your home screen).
      3. Optional: add a second automation for the evening.

      If Affirm isn't in the Open App list on your iOS version, use Show Notification instead as the nudge. Don't use “Open URLs”: that opens Safari, which can't see what's saved in this app.

      Backup

      Everything is saved on this phone only. Export now and then so you don't lose it if the app's storage is cleared.
